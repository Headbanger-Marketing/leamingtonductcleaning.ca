FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

The calls we get every week, answered the way we answer them on the phone.

Is your crew certified and insured?

Both, and documented. Duct cleaning has no provincial trade oversight in Ontario, so the burden falls on the company, and ours carries certification, ongoing training, and full insurance on every residential and commercial job in the Leamington area.

How often do ducts need cleaning around Leamington?

Every two to three years is the local norm. Greenhouse-belt dust, lakeside humidity, and field work push toward the shorter end, and homes that shut down for winter benefit from a start-of-season check.

Do you show the results?

Photos of your duct interiors, before and after, reviewed before we pack up. Around here the before photos are their own argument for the service.

My dryer is slow and running hot. What does that mean?

Almost always a vent filling with lint somewhere between the machine and the hood. We brush the whole run, measure the flow at the end, and tell you honestly if the readings point at the appliance instead.

What does a visit look like, and how long does it take?

Vacuum connected, registers removed and washed, every supply and return run brushed under suction, blower compartment cleaned, and photos taken throughout. Three to four hours covers most homes in town, longer for big farmhouse layouts.

Can you deal with the must when we open the cottage?

Yes. Cleaning removes what the closed-up months left behind, and a sanitizer pass on the clean duct walls handles the rest. Doing it in spring means the whole season starts fresh.

Why does an older house here stay dusty?

Usually return-side leaks in a cellar or crawlspace, feeding the fan a steady supply of dust and damp. If a past cleaning has not held, ask us to test the sealing before repeating anything.

Do you go outside Leamington?

Yes, across Kingsville, Essex, Harrow, Wheatley, and toward Amherstburg, plus the Point Pelee and cottage roads. Call with the address and we will confirm timing.

Do you serve areas outside of Leamington, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Leamington and nearby communities including Kingsville, Essex, Harrow, Wheatley, and the surrounding area.
